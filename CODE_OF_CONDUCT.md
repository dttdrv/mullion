# Code of conduct

This covers Mullion's issues, pull requests, discussions and wiki, and its Discord.

Be decent to each other. Code, results and ideas can be criticised as hard as they deserve; the person who brought
them is left out of it. Being wrong is fine here, and being told so is not an attack.

Not accepted: harassment, slurs, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and going after people
for who they are.

The same goes for how other projects are treated. Mullion is built on DXMT and runs in Wine and CrossOver, whose
developers owe it nothing. Problems with Mullion are reported to Mullion.

Someone who breaks this is told once, and removed from the project's spaces if it goes on. Something that should not
be discussed in public can be sent to the maintainer, at the address on [this profile](https://github.com/dttdrv).
